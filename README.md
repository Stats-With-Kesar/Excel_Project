# Excel Sales Revenue Dashboard
An interactive Excel dashboard analyzing sales revenue across years, regions, countries, item types, and sales channels using slicers for dynamic filtering.

## Overview
This dashboard tracks total sales revenue and cost, with breakdowns by year, region, country, order priority, and item type. Users can filter dynamically using slicers for Sales Channel, Item Type, Region, and Country.

## Dashboard Views
- **Total Revenue by Years** — year-over-year revenue trend
- **Revenue by Year and Region** — regional revenue trend across years (Central, East)
- **Revenue by Items** — revenue comparison between Beverages and Snacks
- **Revenue and Total Cost by Region** — cost vs. revenue comparison across Central and East regions
- **Total Revenue by Order Priority** — revenue share by order priority level
- **Revenue by Countries** — revenue comparison across India and India District

## Filters (Slicers)
- Sales Channel: Offline, Online
- Item Type: Beverages, Snacks
- Region: Central, East
- Country: Illinois, India, India District

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers)
